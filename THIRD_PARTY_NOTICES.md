## Community audio sources

The Mission Control voice clips in app/res/raw are a subset of the community soundboard at
https://github.com/aido727/HD2-buttons-soundboard-app (public/audio/voices). The naming and grouping
also follow https://github.com/BubbleDuckyBro/helldivers-2-stratagem-hero-audio-files. The 29 original
clips came from HDWearStrategy; the other 48 were downloaded from that soundboard. They are game
audio and remain the property of Arrowhead Game Studios AB / Sony Interactive Entertainment.

The 65 voice_*.ogg files shipped in the APK have been pre-gained by +6.02 dB with a 0.891 peak
limiter (tools/apply_voice_gain.py) so that a 200% in-app voice setting can reach twice the 1.2
baseline loudness. This processing does not change the ownership of the underlying recordings.
